    People type in bursts and stop to think, read and scroll after long answers, and prompt at uneven times. Machines draw flat bands of identical height, perfectly spaced ticks, or prompts with no keys before them.

    Rhythm fingerprint

    Each active second against the next one. A person leaves a wide, smeared comet near the corner. An autotyper leaves one tight blob away from it. A jiggler leaves dots on the edges.

    Reading clock

    How long after each answer the next prompt came, against how long the answer was. The pink band is reading at 5 to 60 characters a second. Below it, nobody could have read the answer.
